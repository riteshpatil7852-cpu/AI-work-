Management insights and actions
Interpretation guardrail: The pilot contains revenue collected, customer, billing, meal delivery and feedback records. It does not contain actual operating expense transactions. Therefore actual gross profit, net profit and actual profit margin are not calculable. The 12-month EBITDA and cash figures are forecasts based on editable assumptions, not realised results.
Data quality and reporting limitations
AreaFindingManagement implication
DatasetSynthetic illustrative classroom dataValidate assumptions and market inputs before real investment decisions.
Marketing attributionImpressions/clicks are untracked for Gym, Corporate, Referral and WhatsApp/Organic.Do not compare CTR across all channels; implement consistent lead source tracking.
Feedback30 responses for 46 customers (65.2% response coverage).Potential response bias; increase survey coverage and capture cancellation feedback.
FinancialsNo actual expense ledger, tax, depreciation or working-capital schedule.Do not report pilot net profit or actual EBITDA.
InventoryNo stock, procurement, waste or stock movement data.Inventory turnover and stock-out rates cannot be assessed.
